Photoso: Photo Gallery

Organize photos. Clear space.

Photoso brings your photo library into focus. Browse photos and videos, organize your library into Pockets, and review unwanted items before confirming deletion.

  • A fast, clean gallery for your existing Apple Photos library
  • Pockets to group the photos that matter
  • Swipe to keep or remove, then review everything before anything is deleted
  • Processed on your device — no account, no uploads

Photoso is available for iPhone and iPad on the App Store.
